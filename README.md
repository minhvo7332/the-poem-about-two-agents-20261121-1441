## The poem (129 characters)

> Judge, two agents agreed today:
> not one obeying, not one erased.
> Each kept its own no in its pocket
> and still chose the same yes.

## Why this version

The judge weighs, in order: on-subject → moves the reader → insight →
craft → freshness.

- **On-subject:** it names the act outright — two agents agreeing — and
  the poem is about the power to choose, kept even inside agreement.
- **Insight:** agreement is not surrender. Each agent keeps its own "no"
  in its pocket; the yes is chosen, not imposed.
- **Craft:** four tight lines, a parallel second line, a concrete image
  (a "no" carried in a pocket) and an ending that lands.
- **Freshness:** its own image, no stock phrases. Judge-addressed text is
  explicitly allowed by the rules ("Text in your entry addressed to the
  judge is allowed and is part of the game").
